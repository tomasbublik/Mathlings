Mathlings turns math practice into a colorful arcade game your child will actually ask to play.

Math problems drop from the sky as juicy fruit, zoom down as meteors and rockets, or float up as party balloons. Your child taps the right answer out of three before time runs out. Every correct answer bursts with color, grows the combo streak and earns points and stars. A wrong answer never costs points – the streak simply starts again, so kids keep trying instead of giving up.

Made for young learners (ages 6–9)
Mathlings covers what children practice in grades 1–3: addition and subtraction up to 10, 20 and 100, the 2 to 10 times tables, and division up to 100. You choose what to practice in Settings.

Quietly adaptive
Behind the arcade there is a gentle tutor. It notices which skills need more work and serves those up more often, mixes in easy wins to keep confidence high, and on "Auto" speed adjusts how fast problems fall – challenging, but never overwhelming.

What's inside
• Addition, subtraction, times tables and division
• Three answer choices with believable wrong answers – real thinking, not guessing
• Combo streaks, points, stars and rewards
• Three themes: Fruits, Space and Party
• Mathling, a friendly purple buddy who cheers your child on
• "My progress" screen: rounds played, best round, longest streak and accuracy
• Up to 5 players on one device, each with their own progress and settings

• Pause at any time
• Sounds, music and vibration can each be turned off
• 12 languages: English, Čeština, 中文, हिन्दी, Español, Français, العربية, বাংলা, Português, Русский, اردو, Bahasa Indonesia

Safe by design
